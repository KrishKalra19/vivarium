Reading it: an interval entirely below 0 means vigilance reliably falls as groups grow. The many-eyes prediction needs the **food-adjusted** slope to be negative **with** predators and flat **without** them. 'Rich food' columns are the share of scans on food level ≥2 at each group size; if they climb with group size, group size and food are confounded and the raw slope can't be trusted.
