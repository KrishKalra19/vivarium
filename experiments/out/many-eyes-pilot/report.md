# Many-eyes analysis: many-eyes-pilot

Vigilance = share of feed + vigilant scans spent vigilant (head up and nearly still; head-up travel and fleeing excluded). Slope = change in that share per doubling of group size, fitted per replicate (weighted by scans, group sizes with ≥50 scans), then averaged across replicates with a t-based 95% CI.
